Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. Learn how we handle your information.

1. What Information We Collect

When you use DIMA Deals, we collect:

  • Email Address: Used for account creation and order confirmations
  • Phone Number: For WhatsApp messages and order support
  • Payment Information: Bank transfer details or payment method (processed securely)
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, products viewed, searches performed
  • Device Information: Browser type, IP address, operating system
  • Order History: Products purchased and delivery status

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Deliver your purchased subscriptions and products
  • Process payments and handle refunds
  • Provide customer support
  • Send order confirmations and updates
  • Personalize your experience
  • Improve our services and prevent fraud
  • Send promotional content (if you opt in)

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We Do NOT Share Your Data

We do not sell, share, or disclose your personal information to third parties for marketing or any other purpose without your explicit consent.

The only exceptions:

  • Third-party payment processors (encrypted and secure)
  • Essential service providers (hosting, analytics)
  • Legal requirements (government orders, law enforcement)

4. Data Security

We protect your information through:

  • Encryption: All sensitive data encrypted in transit and at rest
  • Secure Servers: Enterprise-level hosting with security measures
  • Access Control: Only authorized personnel access data
  • Regular Audits: Security assessments and updates
  • SSL Certificate: HTTPS encryption for all website traffic

However, no method of transmission is 100% secure. We work to maintain appropriate security but cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Cookies & Tracking

What are Cookies? Small text files stored on your device to remember your preferences.

How we use them:

  • Remember your logged-in status
  • Store shopping cart contents
  • Track site usage (analytics)
  • Remember dark mode preference
  • Provide personalized recommendations

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect your experience.

6. Your Privacy Rights

GDPR Compliance (EU Users)

If you're in the EU, you have rights regarding your data:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of your data
  • Right to Rectification: Correct inaccurate information
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your data
  • Right to Restriction: Limit processing of your data
  • Right to Portability: Download your data in portable format
  • Right to Object: Opt out of marketing communications

To exercise these rights: Email dimadeals0@gmail.com with your request.

All Users
